Getting Started

What's ready to use in the operations portal for the start of the year. Pick your role below to see just your tools — everything here is live today at admin.mizzentopdayschool.org.

Two things before you start. Sign in with your Mizzentop Google account — no separate password. Every page has the navy menu bar across the top; that's how you move between tools without going back home. If a menu item you expect isn't there, you don't have that permission yet — just ask and it takes a minute to add.

Everyone

Open to every signed-in staff member. These live in the Reference menu.
🗓
Student & Teacher SchedulesStart here
Look up any teacher's or any student's full week, exactly as the master timetable has it — classes, prep, recess/lunch, homeroom and dismissal. Search a student by name, or pick a whole homeroom and print every child's schedule, one per page.
Menu: Reference → Student & Teacher Schedules
👪
Family Directory
Search any family or student and see the household — address, parents/guardians, phones, emails, who's authorized for pickup, and each student's emergency contact. Prints a per-family emergency sheet or a whole-school emergency contact list.
Menu: Reference → Family Directory · Read-only
🚸
Dismissal Staff View
Where every child is going today — buses, car line, activities, and any change a parent called in. This is the screen to check at the end of the day.
Menu: Reference → Dismissal Staff View
🍪
M Card Snack Tracker & School Store
Recording a snack or a store purchase for a student. Anyone can be the one covering the window on a given day, so it's worth knowing where these live — and both flow straight through to that family's monthly bill, so what you enter here is what they're charged.
Menu: Daily Input → M Card Snack Tracker / School Store
🚌
Bus Dashboard
Today's riders by bus route.
Menu: Reference → Bus Dashboard
📊
Attendance Report
Absence, tardy and early-dismissal tallies by trimester, per student or per homeroom. Exports to a spreadsheet; prior years are available from the year picker.
Menu: Reference → Attendance Report
🧩
Scheduler
The master timetable board for the whole school. Everyone can open it and look; for most staff it's view-only — nothing you click can change the live schedule.
Menu: People → Scheduler · View-only unless you're a schedule editor
✍️
Email Signature Generator
Fill in your name and title and it builds a Mizzentop email signature you can paste straight into Gmail. Worth two minutes in the first week.
Menu: Reference → Email Signature Generator

Homeroom teachers

You may also teach subjects — if so, the Subject teacher section below is yours too.
🏫
My ClassroomYour home base
Signing in drops you here. Your own weekly schedule at the top; then your homeroom roster with allergy and medical flags, today's dismissal for each child, and attendance you can take right on the page. Click any student's name to open their weekly schedule.
Menu: My Classroom · This is also what "/" opens for you
✅
Homeroom Attendance
Take it right on your My Classroom page — that's the whole job, and it's the only thing you have to do in here every day. This separate screen is the same thing with more room: present, absent, tardy, early dismissal, excused or not. Either way it's the same record, and it feeds the attendance report and, later, report cards.
Menu: My Classroom, or Daily Input → Homeroom Attendance · Every morning
🖨
Your homeroom's schedules, printed
On the Schedules page choose Homeroom, pick yours, and print — you get one page per student, ready to send home or tape inside a folder in the first week.
Menu: Reference → Student & Teacher Schedules → Homeroom

Subject teachers

Anyone teaching a timetabled class — whether or not you also have a homeroom.
🏫
My ClassroomYour home base
Your weekly schedule plus every section you teach, with its roster, room and student count. Click a student to see their week — useful when you need to find them, or to know why they're pulled out.
Menu: My Classroom · This is also what "/" opens for you
🗓
Your week, printed
Schedules → Teacher → your name gives you the full grid: classes, prep, recess/lunch (with or without duty), Wednesday electives, homeroom and dismissal. Hover any class and its roster pops up. Print it for your desk.
Menu: Reference → Student & Teacher Schedules → Teacher
🔎
Finding a student mid-day
Schedules → Student, search the name, and you see where they are every period — including any in-session support that pulls them from your class, shown in green with the provider's name.
Menu: Reference → Student & Teacher Schedules → Student
✅
Attendance
Daily attendance is taken by the homeroom teacher, not per class. If you don't have a homeroom you have nothing to take — check the Attendance Report if you need a student's history.
Nothing to do daily

Other staff

Tutors, support providers, aides, program staff and helpers. You'll land on the portal home screen rather than a classroom dashboard.
🗓
Your own scheduleNew this year
Every active staff member can now be looked up on the Schedules page — not just classroom teachers. If you run in-session support sessions, they appear on your week with the student, service and room. Choose Teacher, then your name.
Menu: Reference → Student & Teacher Schedules → Teacher
🤝
Special Services Schedule
The during-the-day support grid — periods 1–8 across Monday–Friday. Each session is a staff member, a room, a service (OG tutoring, push-in, pull-out) and either a specific student or "no specific student" when you're pushing into a room. Double-bookings get flagged in red. Filter by staff, service or grade, and print.
Menu: People → Special Services · Needs the Special Services permission
🍎
Daily Ops
Where the day's dismissal gets planned and same-day changes get entered, so they appear on the Dismissal Staff View everyone else reads. One designated person runs this — if that isn't you, you don't need to open it. Phone a change in to them rather than entering it yourself.
Menu: Daily Input → Daily Ops · One person only
📋
Program Attendance
Attendance for tutoring, clubs and enrichment programs — this is what feeds family billing, so it matters that it's taken. Before & Aftercare has its own screen.
Menu: Daily Input → Program Attendance / Before & Aftercare · If you run a program
